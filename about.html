THE WORLD AROUND THE MUSIC

01 / The idea

23

A space for the
whole picture.

Listen to Twenty Three is a music-led portfolio built around three featured tracks: “IG MODEL,” “Queen St,” and “The Highest ft. Stopthis.”

Rather than treating artwork as an afterthought, this project gives each track a visual entry point. The result is a small, navigable archive where titles, imagery and context work together.

The site is intentionally lightweight: original local SVG artwork, custom CSS, and a little JavaScript create an experience that works across phones, tablets and desktop screens without depending on a front-end framework.

02 / The approach

Small details.
Clear intent.

The interface follows three simple principles: make it easy to explore, give the artwork space, and keep the controls in the visitor’s hands.

01◉

Sound first

The featured tracks are easy to find and linked to the artist’s YouTube channel.

02▧

Visual language

Original SVG artwork gives the archive a consistent identity without external image dependencies.

03↔

Built for every screen

Responsive layouts, visible keyboard focus, and clear labels support different ways of browsing.
